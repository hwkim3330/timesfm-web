TimesFM 2.5 in your browser

Google's TimesFM 2.5 (200M) converted to ONNX (8-bit weights, ~250 MB, cached after first load), run locally with onnxruntime-web on WebGPU (WASM fallback). Nothing is uploaded.

Series (comma/space separated, oldest first; up to 512 points used). Default: a synthetic seasonal series.

Band = q10–q90, line = median. Apache-2.0 model by Google Research; see repo.
